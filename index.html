FAQ

Questions, answered

Which documents do I need for an income tax return?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 or income details, bank interest certificates, Form 26AS/AIS, and investment proofs for deductions. We tell you exactly what applies to you.

Should I file GST monthly or quarterly?

Eligible small businesses can opt for quarterly filing under the QRMP scheme. We check your turnover and recommend what suits you.

Can you help with a notice from the Income Tax or GST department?

Yes. Send us the notice and we will explain what it means, the deadline, and the reply needed.

Do you handle capital gains from shares and mutual funds?

Yes. Share your broker's capital gains statement and we compute short-term and long-term gains and the tax due.

Do I need to visit an office?

No. Everything runs on WhatsApp, email and phone. Payment is online too.

Is the firm a government body?

No. We are an independent professional practice and are not affiliated with any government department.
